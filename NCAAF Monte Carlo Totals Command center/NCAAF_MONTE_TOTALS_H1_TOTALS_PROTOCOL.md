# First-Half Totals Protocol

First-half totals must be generated from the actual halftime state in the sequential simulation.

Do not accept:

```text
H1 total = 0.5 * full-game total
```

without a blocking or severe review flag.

The Trading Agent must check:

- H1 simulation distribution;
- first-half possession/tempo assumptions;
- early-season uncertainty;
- weather impact timing;
- H1/full-game consistency;
- correlation with FG totals and team totals.
