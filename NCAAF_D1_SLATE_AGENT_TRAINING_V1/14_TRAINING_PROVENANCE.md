# Training Provenance

This package is based on the actual 2026-09-03 NCAAF Week 1 capture and recovery workflow.

Preserved lessons:
- 11/11 modeled results captured;
- target inventory anchored to the frozen NCAAF handoff;
- FG, team-total, and H1 markets handled separately;
- closing labels conservative;
- first-pass gaps retried;
- SportsbookReview historical matrices used for FG/H1 recovery;
- The Odds Gap used for historical pre-kick archive evidence;
- ATS.io used for team totals only when sportsbook identity could be resolved;
- mixed-book, one-sided, and corrupt evidence rejected;
- unresolved exact-book closes left as `DATA GAP`;
- Trading Agent remains owner of CLV, grading, ROI, and calibration conclusions.

The worked-example files are included for regression testing and supervised reference.
